# Small Business Excel Toolkit (India)

Storefront / landing page for a pack of 4 ready-to-use Excel templates for small Indian businesses:

1. Invoice & Billing Tracker
2. Business Expense Tracker (with automatic category summary)
3. Simple Inventory Tracker (with reorder alerts)
4. Customer & Leads Tracker (mini CRM)

The product files themselves are delivered by email after purchase — they are intentionally **not** committed to this public repository.

The landing page is served with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.
